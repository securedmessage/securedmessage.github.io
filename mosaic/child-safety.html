Child Safety Standards

Effective November 3, 2026

Mosaic, made by RM Software, has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards explain what's prohibited, how to report it, and what we do when we find it.

What's prohibited

On Mosaic, no one may post, send, share, request or link to:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including drawn, animated or AI-generated images of minors.
  • Any content that sexualizes a minor, including sexualized comments about a child.
  • Grooming: contacting, befriending or manipulating a minor for sexual purposes, or trying to move a minor to private or offline contact for those purposes.
  • Sextortion, or threatening to share intimate images of a minor.
  • Trafficking, or advertising or soliciting the sexual exploitation of a minor.
  • Content that promotes, praises or gives instructions for any of the above.

Mosaic is for people 16 and older. Accounts we learn belong to anyone under 16 are removed.

How to report

  • In the app: open the ⋯ menu on any post, reel, comment, message or profile, choose Report, then Child safety concern.
  • By email: write to 540vdc@gmail.com with the username and a description. Don't attach or forward the material itself.
  • You can also block the account from their profile so they can't contact you.
If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number first. In the United States you can also report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678.

What we do

  • Child safety reports are reviewed before any other kind of report.
  • We remove violating content and permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • We report apparent CSAM to NCMEC as required by U.S. law (18 U.S.C. § 2258A), and to the relevant authorities in other countries where required.
  • We preserve information as required by law and cooperate with law enforcement investigations.

Safety features

  • Private accounts, so only approved followers can see your posts.
  • Blocking, which stops someone from finding your profile, seeing your posts or messaging you.
  • Reporting on every post, reel, comment, message and profile.

Contact

Our child safety point of contact is RM Software at 540vdc@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
