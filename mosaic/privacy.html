Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Mosaic is a photo, video and messaging app made by RM Software ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Mosaic collects, how it's used, who can see it, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email 540vdc@gmail.com.

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, password, name, username, and, if you add them, a bio and profile photo. Passwords are stored by our sign-in provider in a scrambled (hashed) form that we can't read.
  • What you share: posts (photos, videos and captions), reels, stories, comments, likes, saves, follows, follow requests, and the people you tag.
  • Messages: the text, photos, voice messages, shared posts, story replies and reactions you send in one-to-one and group chats.
  • Safety reports: the reason and any details you give when you report a post, comment, message or account, and the accounts you block.

Information collected as you use Mosaic

  • Activity status: when you were last active, if "Show activity status" is on in Settings. Turning it off stops this.
  • Read status: when you last read each chat, used to show "Seen".
  • Technical records: our hosting provider keeps routine server logs, such as IP address, device or browser type and request times, to run and secure the service.
  • Camera and microphone: used only when you choose to take a photo, record a video or record a voice message. Mosaic does not access them in the background.

Mosaic does not use advertising identifiers, ad networks, analytics trackers, your location, or your contacts.

How we use information

  • To run Mosaic: create your account, show your posts and stories to the people you choose, deliver messages, and show notifications in the app.
  • To keep people safe: review reports, enforce our Terms of Use, and remove content or accounts that break them.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Who can see what

  • Your profile (name, username, photo and bio) is visible to people signed in to Mosaic, except people you have blocked.
  • Posts, reels, stories, and your follower lists are visible to all signed-in Mosaic users if your account is public, or only to followers you approve if your account is private. Stories disappear from view after 24 hours.
  • Messages are visible only to the people in that chat.
  • Uploaded photos, videos and voice messages are stored at long, hard-to-guess web addresses. Anyone who has the exact address of a file can open it, so don't share links to files you want to keep private.
Messages are protected in transit (HTTPS) and stored encrypted by our hosting provider, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. As the operator of the service, RM Software can technically access stored data. We only do so to run the service, respond to reports, or when required by law.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores accounts, posts, messages and files and handles sign-in, on servers in the United States.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the web version of Mosaic and these pages.
  • Google Play distributes the Android app under Google's own privacy policy.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete a post, comment, story or message, it's removed from Mosaic right away. When you delete your account, your profile and everything you posted or sent are deleted, including your uploaded files. Copies may remain in our hosting provider's backups for up to 30 days before they're overwritten.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile, and delete your posts, stories, comments and messages at any time.
  • Make your account private, turn off activity status, block people, and remove followers in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete account, or on the web at Delete your Mosaic account.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, or ask a question about it, by emailing 540vdc@gmail.com.

Age requirement

Mosaic is for people 16 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 16, we delete it. If you believe a child is using Mosaic, email us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll let you know in the app.
